# Auto-Cart Product Principles

**CRITICAL DIRECTIVE FOR ALL AGENTS AND DEVELOPERS:**

1. **This is a REAL startup for REAL customers.**
2. Do NOT treat this as a "hackathon project" or a "demo". 
3. Do NOT suggest generating fake "seed data" just to make charts look pretty. Real dashboards start at 0. We earn our metrics through real transactions.
4. Every line of code, every architectural decision, and every security measure must be built for a production environment scaling to millions of real B2B transactions.
5. The SDKs (`@autocart/sdk`, `@autocart/ai-tools`) are real packages meant for global distribution.
6. The cryptography (HMAC-SHA256, Webhook validation) is not a simulation. It is protecting real money.
